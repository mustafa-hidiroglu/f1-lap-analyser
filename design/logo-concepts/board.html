What we heard

The brief

A lap analysis tool for Formula 1 telemetry: two drivers' laps on synced speed, throttle and brake traces, a delta-time line, a track map coloured by who is faster, lap ratings against the field and a physics-simulated perfect lap. Native macOS app, Windows app and static web app. The mark should tell a story, be simple yet emotionally complicated, and feel as if F1 and Apple had a child.

preciserestrainedlongingfasthuman

Success: one idea per mark, a story in two sentences, tension inside the simplicity, reads at 16 px and in one colour, sits naturally in a macOS dock. No cars, helmets, wheels or fruit; not two racing lines through a corner (the current icon).

Concept A

Ghost

You (the solid disc) and the ghost of your better lap (the ring) a hundredth ahead. They never touch.

  • Two primitives, one gap: Apple-level restraint, reads at 16 px
  • The ghost car is how F1 visualises the chase; here it is your own better self
  • Tension: presence vs. absence, almost touching, forever

Recommendation & next steps

Our recommendation

Ghost - the simplest form with the deepest story. Two shapes, one gap; it reads at 16 px, survives one colour, feels at home in a macOS dock, and it is the app's whole purpose (chasing a better version of a lap) in one glance.

  • Pick a direction (or tell me what you like in each)
  • Refine geometry, colour and small-size cut
  • Replace the app icon (Mac .icns, Windows .ico, web favicon)
